Sailors from Navy Talent Acquisition Group (NTAG) Ohio River Valley (ORV) presented Ace Alston, a senior and defensive back at Anderson High School, with his 2027 Navy All-American Bowl jersey during a ceremony held on campus before his classmates, teammates, friends, family, and faculty, Oct. 6, in Cincinnati.
