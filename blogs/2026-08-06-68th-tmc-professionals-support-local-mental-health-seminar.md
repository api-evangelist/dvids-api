On July 22, 2026, behavioral health personnel from the 68th Theater Medical Command (68th TMC) as well as the unit’s chaplain as part of their regular community outreach program participated in a mental health and wellness seminar hosted by the military and civilian staff at Stars and Stripes Europe & Expeditionary at their headquarters at Kleber Kaserne in Kaiserslautern, Germany.
