KOTA KINABALU, Malaysia – During Pacific Partnership 2026 (PP26), the humanitarian and disaster management mission’s medical line of effort (LOE) personnel have bolstered host nations’ healthcare capacity and crisis readiness by hosting public health fairs, joint medical symposiums, and community outreach events, offering comprehensive health screenings and preventive care education in four national communities across the Indo-Pacific.
