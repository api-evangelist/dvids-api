In a major step supporting the Army’s strategic priorities of recruitment, retention, and force transformation, service members stationed at Fort Gordon and around the world will have more opportunities to apply military training toward graduate degrees in cybersecurity and intelligence through a renewed partnership between the U.S. Army Cyber Center of Excellence (CCoE) and Augusta University (AU).
