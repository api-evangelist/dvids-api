HONIARA, Solomon Islands — 20 August 2026 — U.S. Army medical professionals from Tripler Army Medical Center partnered with the U.S. Army Pacific (USARPAC) Oceania Engagement Team (OET) to conduct a two-day medical civic action program (MEDCAP) in Honiara, Solomon Islands, that provided hearing evaluations to over 40 members of the community.
