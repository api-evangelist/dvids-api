Field Training Exercise Big Trouble brought six Air Force EOD teams to Limestone Hills to train in a simulated deployed environment. Over the week, technicians worked scenarios spanning the threats they could meet downrange, such as improvised explosive devices, unexploded ordnance, weapons caches, route denial and chemical agents.
