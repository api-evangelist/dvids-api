FORT MCCLELLAN, Ala. — Soldiers of the 167th Theater Sustainment Command participated in a Wide Area Search (PER-213) course Aug 6-8. This course is a Department of Homeland Security (DHS) / Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) grant funded course provided by Texas A&M Engineering Extension Service (TEEX).
