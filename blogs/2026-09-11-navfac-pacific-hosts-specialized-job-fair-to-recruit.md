JOINT BASE PEARL HARBOR-HICKAM, Hawaii – Naval Facilities Engineering Systems Command (NAVFAC) Pacific hosted a successful job fair Sept. 10 at the Ala Moana Hotel in Honolulu, Hawaii in an effort to recruit and hire permanent, full-time civilian positions across the Pacific area of operations.
