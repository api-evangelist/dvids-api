HOLLOMAN AIR FORCE BASE, N.M. – The 49th Wing hosted National POW/MIA Recognition Day on Holloman Air Force Base, New Mexico, in remembrance and honor of former prisoners of war and those missing in action, Sept. 17-18, 2026.
