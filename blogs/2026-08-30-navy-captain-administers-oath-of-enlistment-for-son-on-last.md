Capt. Rich Sussman administers the Oath of Enlistment for his son, Future Sailor Matthew Sussman, at Albany Military Entrance Processing Station. The occasion marked a unique intersection of career milestones for the family, as Sussman concluded his 35-year Navy career the same day his son shipped to bootcamp.
