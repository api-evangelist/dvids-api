Soldiers with the U.S. Army Reserve's 351st Civil Affairs Command conducted Operation Sunset Canopy Sept. 11 at Joint Forces Training Base Los Alamitos, a tactical airborne and ground insertion exercise that showcased the command's Civil Affairs and Psychological Operations capabilities while honoring the paratroopers and airborne units who built and sustained its jump legacy.
