Airmen assigned to the 149th Medical Group Detachment 1 demonstrate disaster response readiness during a 6th CBRN Enhanced Response Force Package evaluation exercise at Disaster City in College Station, Texas. The exercise tested the medical element’s ability to provide sustained emergency care, integrate with Army National Guard response elements and support civil authorities during a complex domestic emergency.
