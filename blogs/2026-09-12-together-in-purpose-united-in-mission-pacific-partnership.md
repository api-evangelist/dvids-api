KOTA KINABALU, Malaysia – From the shores of Dong Hoi, Vietnam, to the coast of Sibolga, Indonesia, across Dili, Timor-Leste, and now into Kota Kinabalu, Malaysia, Pacific Partnership 2026 (PP26) has brought together multinational military personnel from eight partner nations in support of the four lines of effort (LOE) during the humanitarian assistance and disaster management preparedness mission’s 20th anniversary in the Indo-Pacific, with each service member contributing a unique specialty or skill set toward a shared mission.
