Against the backdrop of America’s approaching 250th birthday, the “Chargers” of Helicopter Sea Combat Squadron (HSC) 26 at Naval Station Norfolk unveiled a striking custom-painted tail and side panels of their MH-60S Seahawk helicopter honoring the spirit, sacrifice, and strength of the United States before International Naval Review (INR) 250 in New York City.
