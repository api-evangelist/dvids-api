YOKOTA AIR BASE, Japan — Students assigned to the Japan Air Self-Defense Force 1st Technical School visited Yokota Air Base during a bilateral immersion tour designed to strengthen the U.S.-Japan alliance through professional engagement and mission familiarization. Throughout the visit, students toured key facilities, observed aircraft and mission capabilities, and met with U.S. Air Force Airmen to learn about Yokota's role in the Indo-Pacific.
