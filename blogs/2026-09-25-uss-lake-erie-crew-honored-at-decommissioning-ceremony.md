NAVAL BASE SAN DIEGO—Ticonderoga-class guided-missile cruiser USS Lake Erie (CG 70) and the crews who have manned it, were recognized during a decommissioning ceremony, celebrating 33 years of faithful service, held at Naval Base San Diego, Sept. 25.
