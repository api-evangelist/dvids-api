Soldiers from the 25th Infantry Division and student-athletes from the University of Hawaii Rainbow Warrior football team came together on July 24, 2026, for a day of shared challenges designed to build teamwork and strengthen community bonds. The event, hosted at Schofield Barracks, saw players and Soldiers tackling military obstacle courses and leadership challenges side by side.
