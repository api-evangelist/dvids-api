The Mission and Installation Contracting Command closed fiscal year 2026 with more than 21,450 contracting actions valued at approximately $6.8 billion, including $2.9 billion awarded to American small businesses, as the command expanded its role in Army modernization and innovative acquisition. The command also managed more than 299,000 Government Purchase Card transactions valued at more than $520 million, providing additional purchasing power support to Soldiers, installations and mission partners across the Army.
