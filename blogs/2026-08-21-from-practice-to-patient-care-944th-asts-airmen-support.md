SAN PEDRO SULA, Honduras — U.S. Airmen from the 944th Aeromedical Staging Squadron completed a two-week Medical Readiness Training Exercise in Honduras, gaining hands-on experience providing patient care in resource-limited environments alongside U.S. and Honduran medical professionals.
