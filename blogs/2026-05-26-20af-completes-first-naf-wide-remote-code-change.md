Twentieth Air Force has achieved a significant milestone with the successful completion of OLYMPIC STEP 2024, which marked the first-ever NAF-wide Remote Code Change (RCC). This year’s operation saw missile combat crew members executing secure transmissions of keys and codes directly from the Launch Control Center (LCC), eliminating the need for physical installations by maintenance teams at each Launch Facility (LF).
