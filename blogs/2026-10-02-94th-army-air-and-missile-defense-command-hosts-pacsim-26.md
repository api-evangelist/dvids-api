JOINT BASE PEARL HARBOR-HICKAM, Hawaii -- Air and missile defense professionals from the U.S., Japanese, and Australian militaries (respectively) gathered at Joint Base Pearl Harbor-Hickam, Hawaii, Sept. 21-26, 2026, for the Pacific Simulation for Integrated Air and Missile Defense Modernization simulation event.
