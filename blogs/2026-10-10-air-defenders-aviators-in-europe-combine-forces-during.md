On Oct. 6, 2026, U.S. Army soldiers assigned to the 5th Battalion, 4th Air Defense Artillery Regiment, 52nd Air Defense Artillery Brigade, worked with soldiers from the 2nd Battalion, 159th Aviation Regiment, 12th Combat Aviation Brigade, at the Grafenwoehr Training Area to showcase connectivity and interoperability capabilities first assessed by both units in the spring.
