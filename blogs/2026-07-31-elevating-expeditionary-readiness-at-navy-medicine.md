Navy Medicine Readiness and Training Command (NMRTC) Patuxent River is sharpening expeditionary trauma care skills through regular "Warrior Wednesday" training evolutions. Anchored in Tactical Combat Casualty Care (TCCC) principles, the command-wide curriculum covers physical trauma algorithms, acute mental health triage, and complex casualty evacuation drills. The continuous training builds muscle memory across the command as personnel prepare for a major APEX training event in October bringing together all four NMRTC Patuxent River sites.
