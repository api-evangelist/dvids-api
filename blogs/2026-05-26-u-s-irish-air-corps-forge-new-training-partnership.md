A delegation of Airmen from Twentieth Air Force and Air Force Global Strike Command visited the Casement Aerodrome in Baldonnel, Dublin to coordinate a training partnership agreement, June 22-29, 2024. This new partnership builds on the cooperation between the U.S. Air Force and the Irish Air Corps, aiming to enhance operational knowledge and flight safety through shared training experiences.
