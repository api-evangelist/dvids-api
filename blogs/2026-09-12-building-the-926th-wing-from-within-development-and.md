The 926th Wing’s Development and Training Flight prepares newly enlisted reservists for basic military training by teaching foundational drill, customs, and physical fitness standards before they report to duty. Through hands-on shadowing of operational units and peer-to-peer mentorship, the program eases the transition to military life and integrates recruits directly into their future squadrons.
