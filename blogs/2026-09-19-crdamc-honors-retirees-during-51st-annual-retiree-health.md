Darnall Army Medical Center hosted the 51st Annual Retiree Health Fair and Fort Hood Retiree Appreciation Day opening ceremony Sept. 19, bringing together military retirees, eligible beneficiaries and community partners for a day focused on health, resources and continued connection to the military community.
