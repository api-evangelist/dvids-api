U.S. Army Sgt. Munk Munkhzaya, a finance technician assigned to the 125th Finance Battalion, 8th Theater Sustainment Command, returned home wearing the uniform of the country he now serves. During Exercise Khaan Quest 2026, he will also reenlist in the U.S. Army in his native country, making the ceremony a full-circle moment in his military career.
