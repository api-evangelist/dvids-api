A couple dozen members of the Navy Reserve Center Madison Chiefs’ Mess visited Fort McCoy’s Commemorative Area on Sept. 16 for a scavenger hunt designed to challenge and engage newly selected Navy chief petty officers while giving them an opportunity to learn about the history of the installation.
