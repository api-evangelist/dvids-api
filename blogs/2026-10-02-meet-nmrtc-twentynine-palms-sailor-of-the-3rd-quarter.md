Hospital Corpsman 2nd Class Courtlynn Stinson, an X-ray technician assigned to Navy Medicine Readiness and Training Detachment Bridgeport, was selected as Navy Medicine Readiness and Training Command Twentynine Palms Sailor of the Quarter for the third quarter of 2026. A Mobile, Alabama, native, Stinson supports warfighter readiness through diagnostic imaging and patient care while also serving in several leadership roles at the detachment. Her contributions include resolving a longstanding technology system issues, supporting CT and X-ray operations during a one-year temporary additional duty
