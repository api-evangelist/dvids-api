U.S. Army Garrison Stuttgart’s Directorate of Emergency Services hosted a National Night Out Event on August 4 at Panzer, Kelley, Patch and Robinson housing areas; the event yielded more than 200 community contacts. Military Police performed “slow rolls” as they moved through the neighborhoods, handing out candy and leaflets about back-to-school safety.
