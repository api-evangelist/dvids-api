WIESBADEN, Germany — Military working dog handlers, Army combat medics, civilian K9 handlers and security personnel from installations across Germany and Belgium gathered at the Wiesbaden Veterinary Treatment Facility for a training event focused on Tactical Combat Casualty Care and UH-60 Black Hawk familiarization.
