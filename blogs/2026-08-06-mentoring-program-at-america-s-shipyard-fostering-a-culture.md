Norfolk Naval Shipyard (NNSY) officially re-established the Mentorship Program with the signing of NAVSHIPYDNORINST 12411.1A on June 17, 2026. This updated instruction introduces a formal structure designed to foster personal and professional development for all NNSY civilian employees, building a culture of lifelong learning crucial for retaining a highly skilled workforce.
