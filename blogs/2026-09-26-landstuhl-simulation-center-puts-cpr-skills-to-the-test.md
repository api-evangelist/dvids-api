Anyone who has ever watched a medical drama knows how effortless CPR can look on screen. But as dozens of military and civilian personnel recently discovered, real-life lifesaving is an entirely different story. Landstuhl Regional Medical Center Simulation Center hosted a fast-paced Chest Compression Challenge Sept.17 outside the Hill Top Café as part of Healthcare Simulation Week.
