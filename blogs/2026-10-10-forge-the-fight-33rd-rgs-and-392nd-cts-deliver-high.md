Forge the Fight: 33rd RGS and 392nd CTS Deliver High-Fidelity Cyber Training By: Space Training and Readiness Command Public Affairs In a successful demonstration of operational cyber readiness, the 33rd Range and Aggressor Squadron executed Magenta Stars in partnership with the 392nd Combat Training Squadron late last month. The exercise put the 69th Cyberspace Squadron’s operators through rigorous, high-fidelity threat scenarios testing their ability to safeguard operational space networks.
