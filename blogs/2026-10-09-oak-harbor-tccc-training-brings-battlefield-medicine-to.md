Navy Medicine Readiness and Training Command Oak Harbor (NMRTCOH) brought battlefield medicine one step closer to reality Oct. 1, concluding its two-week Tactical Combat Casualty Care (TCCC) course with a field exercise featuring a live helicopter evacuation supported by Naval Air Station Whidbey Island Search and Rescue (SAR).
