Defense Logistics Agency Energy’s new commander, Navy Rear Adm. Timothy Brown, addressed the agency’s workforce during his first all-hands meeting, outlining his strategic roadmap, upcoming administrative changes and his vision for developing the next generation of the workforce. More than 350 employees attended the all-hands in the McNamara Headquarters Complex auditorium, where Brown and Deputy Commander Emily Vogeler discussed employee evaluation changes, artificial intelligence implementation and workforce expectations, while also recognizing employee milestones and achievements.
