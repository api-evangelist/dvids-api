Fort Knox Schools will undergo some changes this year that are expected to foster a more focused learning environment and greater growth opportunities. The changes, according to school administrators, are designed by the Department of War Education Activity to focus centrally on students. Examples include no use of cell phones during school hours, understanding of when and how to use artificial intelligence and weaving classical education back into the curriculum.
