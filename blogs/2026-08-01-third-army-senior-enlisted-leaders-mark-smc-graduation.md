Senior enlisted leaders from U.S. Army Central (ARCENT) & Third Army recently attended the Sergeants Major Course graduation during a visit to the U.S. Army Noncommissioned Officer Academy (USANCOA) at Fort Bliss, Texas as well as visited with the 32nd Army Air & Missile Defense Command to discuss air defense readiness.
