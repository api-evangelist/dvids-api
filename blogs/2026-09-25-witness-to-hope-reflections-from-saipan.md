A first-person reflection on deploying to Saipan with the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers after Super Typhoon Sinlaku. Through the stories of Soldiers, engineers, partner agencies and local families, the author witnesses the power of service, resilience and human connection as communities begin rebuilding their homes—and their hope.
