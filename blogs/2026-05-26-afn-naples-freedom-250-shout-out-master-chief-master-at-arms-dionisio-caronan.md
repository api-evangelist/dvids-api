260525-N-PS829-2002 Nettuno, Italy (May 25, 2026)Master Chief Master-at-Arms Dionisio Caronan, assigned to Commander, Navy Region Europe, Africa, Central Command delivers a shout-out in honor of the 250th birthday of the United States at Sicily-Rome American Cemetery, Nettuno, Italy.
