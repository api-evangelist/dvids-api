Sixteen Soldiers from the 512th Movement Control Company returned to the homes in the Caribbean after an 11-month deployment to the CENTCOM area, greeted warmly by family and the community. Their roles as Transportation Management Coordinators were crucial to military operations in the Middle East, and their homecoming is celebrated just in time for Independence Day.
