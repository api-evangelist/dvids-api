KAISERSLAUTERN, Germany — Twenty-five years after the terrorist attacks of Sept. 11, 2001, Soldiers and community members gathered at Panzer Kaserne, Kaiserslautern, Germany on Sept. 9, 2026, to remember the lives lost, honor the first responders who answered the call and reflect on the generations of service that followed.
