JOINT BASE ELMENDORF-RICHARDSON, Alaska – The Alaska Rescue Coordination Center has opened a search and rescue mission Aug. 20 in response to a Federal Aviation Administration alert notification regarding an aircraft incident near the Cape Newenham Long Range Radar Site (LRRS).
