At Norfolk Naval Shipyard (NNSY), the sight of a safety hat approaching the waterfront can sometimes bring work to a sudden halt. Mechanics and technicians often brace for an inspection or a reprimand, instinctively pausing their tasks. However, the industrial hygienists of the Occupational Safety, Health, and Environmental (OSHE) Office (Code 106.21) want the workforce to know they are allies in helping to meet the shipyard mission.
