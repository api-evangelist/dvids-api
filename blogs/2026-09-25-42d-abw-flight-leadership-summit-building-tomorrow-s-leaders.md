MAXWELL AIR FORCE BASE, Ala. -- Flight leaders from across Maxwell Air Force Base met Sept. 22-25 for the 42d Air Base Wing Flight Leadership Summit, a course designed to strengthen leadership at the flight level and prepare Airmen to develop teams, make decisions and execute the mission under increasingly complex conditions.
