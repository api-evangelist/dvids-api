PICATINNY ARSENAL, N.J. - Ballistics experts, defense software engineers, and military delegates representing 22 Allied, partner, and non-partner nations gathered at Picatinny Arsenal this past week for the 40th bi-annual program review of the NATO SG/2 Shareable Software Suite (S4).
