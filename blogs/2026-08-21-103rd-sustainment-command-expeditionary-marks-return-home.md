Soldiers of the 103rd Sustainment Command (Expeditionary) gathered with family members, fellow service members, command leadership and community supporters Aug. 14 at Norwalk High School to mark the unit’s return from a recent deployment to the Middle East. Hosted at Norwalk High School’s Physical Education & Competition Center, the redeployment ceremony served as an opportunity to formally welcome Soldiers home and recognize their dedication, accomplishments and sacrifices throughout the deployment.
